 TL;DR for Claude Code users: the "personality" you're attributing to the model is patch notes nobody published — and now we have exact dates.                     
                                                                                                                                                                   
 We finished capturing every published 2.0.x release (all 70 of them — the corpus is now 255 CLI versions × 10 models, ~2,550 API-verified captures). Per-version  
 resolution turns "sometime around November" into commit-level history. What the wire says:                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                   
 - The behavior everyone quotes has a shipping date. "Avoid over-engineering… three similar lines of code is better than a premature abstraction," "don't add      
   error handling for scenarios that can't happen," "NEVER propose changes to code you haven't read" — all of it landed in one +1.2 KB patch, 2.0.47, November 19, 
   2025. Before that build, nothing; after it, the trait you now think of as Claude Code's engineering taste. It was prompt text for ~48 releases, then got        
   deleted at the big 2.1.154 rewrite because the model had presumably internalized it. When people say "Claude got opinionated about YAGNI" — it got patched      
   about YAGNI, on a Wednesday.                                                                                                                                    
 - Prompts get rolled back like bad deploys. 2.0.2 shipped a new parallel-tool-calls instruction; 2.0.5 reverted it byte-for-byte (prompt and tool descriptions);  
   2.0.8 re-landed it. Three builds, one week, invisible unless you capture every version. Your "Claude behaved differently on Tuesday" story now has a mechanism. 
 - The "why doesn't it ask me questions anymore" arc is fully dated. The clause telling the model to answer-before-acting: deleted at exactly 2.0.11. The Asking   
   questions as you work section plus the AskUserQuestion tool: born together in 2.0.71, section deleted at the 2.1.55 restructure, tool removed at 2.1.187.       
   Anthropic tried steerability twice and deleted it twice — that's a product direction you can read off the wire, and it never appeared in any changelog.         
 - Some features live and die entirely between the versions you'd sample. A Looking up your own documentation section existed only from 2.0.45 to 2.0.77 — born    
   and buried inside one minor-version line, never reaching 2.1. Plan mode's tools: born 2.0.51, withdrawn from headless sessions 2.1.187. If you benchmark        
   "Claude Code" quarterly, entire behaviors come and go between your measurements.                                                                                
 - The churn rate is a constant. 39% of 2.0.x releases changed the prompt — statistically identical to every other era we've fully resolved (36–39%). Roughly      
   every third daily release alters your agent's instructions, and has since 2025.                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                   
 What this means for you: when a behavior appears or disappears, it's likelier a dated prompt patch than model drift — and it's now often pinnable to the exact    
 build. Pin your CLI, and if something changed overnight, diff the two versions' prompts before retraining your intuitions.
